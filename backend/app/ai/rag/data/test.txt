## About Aroku

Aroku is a software company focused on building AI-powered business assistants.

The Aroku support assistant can help customers with questions about products, orders, appointments, refunds, and company policies.

## Refund Policy

Customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase.

## Shipping Policy

Standard shipping usually takes between 5 and 7 business days.

## Appointments

Appointments can be scheduled during the company's working hours.

## AI Assistant

The AI assistant uses retrieval augmented generation to retrieve relevant company information before generating an answer.
